## Fault codes: ZERO (the P0043 story)
Two days of "stored fault codes: P0043, P0043" on BOTH cars were a parser
artifact, not car faults. On CAN each module answers mode 03 with
`43 <count> <pairs>`; several modules with no codes produce the stream
`43 00 43 00 43 00`, which the old K-line-style decoder read as data
pairs - and `00 43` decodes to exactly P0043. Fixed in carwatch/elm327.py
(decode_dtc_reply, CAN-aware, unit-tested); verified live on the car
13:00 UTC: `dtcs: []`. The E 300e has no stored fault codes.
